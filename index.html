Grab beats block. While holding, press grab or forward to throw, or press back to throw behind you. If you get grabbed, press grab fast to break free. Power smack is slow but breaks a block.

Online: you each use the Player 1 keys (or arrows with K L M ,) on your own computer.

Hit your opponent to fill your super meter. 1 bar unlocks power 1, 2 bars unlock power 2, and a full meter unlocks the ultimate. Game controllers also work. Press Esc to pause.
